      Thing to Do

      Grand Prismatic Overlook Trail

      Yellowstone National Park

      This trail gradually climbs 105 feet over 0.6 miles from the Fairy Falls Trailhead to a view looking down on Grand Prismatic Spring and the Midway Geyser Basin.

      Trailheads: Park 1 mile (1.6 km) south of Midway Geyser Basin at the Fairy Falls Parking Lot. Parking is very limited.

      Details
      Duration
      1-2 Hours
      This is a 1.2-mile (1.9-kilometer) there-and-back trail that begins and ends at the same trailhead.
      Activity
      Hiking
      Easy hike
      Pets Allowed
      No
      Activity Fee
      No
      Entrance fees may apply, see Fees & Passes information.
      Location
      Midway Geyser Basin
      This trail is located in the western part of the park, with the trailhead south of Midway Geyser Basin.
      Reservations
      No
      Season
      Winter, Summer, Fall
      Time of Day
      Any Time
