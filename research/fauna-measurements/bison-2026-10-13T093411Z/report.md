Caveats:
- Landmarks come from side-view profiles of the mesh, so fur and horns are part of the silhouette.
- Shoulder height is the highest point between 15 and 55 percent of the body length from the nose; check its position in profile.svg.
- Mass is reported only for a closed mesh, from volume times an assumed density of 1000 kg/m3.
- Maximum recorded mass 1724 kg, a semidomestic bull (Meagher 1986, citing Hebbring 1978).
- Hair length on shoulders and hump 65 to 160 mm, chaps 150 to 190 mm, beard about 300 mm (Meagher 1986), so a furred mesh silhouette can stand above skin-level measurements.
- Condylobasal skull length 519 mm for one adult male (YNP 11661) and 460 mm for one adult female (YNP 12050); single specimens, not ranges.
