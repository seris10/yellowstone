# Phase D: Snow Accumulation, Melt Dynamics, and Seasonal State (Scientific Report)

## Deliverable
A single markdown report describing snow model physics (degree-day or energy-balance), thermal exclusion mapping, seasonal state transitions, and coupling to weather, soil moisture, vegetation phenology, and insect life cycles.

## Equations and Relationships
- Snow accumulation/melt: degree-day (base temperature + melt factor) or energy-balance (solar/longwave/sensible/latent heat fluxes) with aspect, canopy, and wind modifiers.
- Thermal exclusion: thermal ground (hydrothermal apron/pools) excluded from uniform snow blanket; snow depth = 0 at thermal zones, positive elsewhere.
- Seasonal state: phenology stage (spring/summer/fall/winter) driven by weather temperature + snow depth + soil moisture; biomass dynamics and insect activity respond to season transition.

## Report Sections
1. Snow model equations and coefficient sources (calibration data or illustrative labeling required).
2. Thermal exclusion mapping and visual representation criteria.
3. Seasonal transition rules linking weather, snow, soil moisture, phenology, vegetation biomass, and insect activity.
4. Validation: apron bare, forest snow-covered, reload preserves season state.
