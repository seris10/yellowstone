Geography and scientific references

MODEL SCOPE

Yellowstone at Midway

The model connects river heat transport, hydrothermal processes, snow and soil water, and ecological responses around Midway Geyser Basin and the Firehole River.

Inputs and interpretation

Geographic inputs define the mapped channel and terrain. Upstream discharge and temperature are prescribed. Channel widths, submerged depths, ecological rates, and many local parameters are authored.

Equations and exchange definitions describe the model structure. Conditional exchanges depend on the selected configuration. The river view replays saved numerical results; its playback controls change viewing time.

Scientific scope

Numerical conservation checks do not establish field accuracy. Local ecological forecasts, organism counts, and submerged bathymetry remain unvalidated. Unknown uncertainty remains unquantified.

Data are extracted from the checked model generation selected by the Yellowstone environment. The snapshot updates when the site is rebuilt.
