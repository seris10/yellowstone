| Item | Verdict | Implementation and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed domain membership | implemented | Baseline: `runtime.py` built `self.domains` once in `__init__`. `add_domain()` requires the domain clock at the frontier, orders coupled domains upstream first and records a membership entry; `remove_domain()` keeps at least one domain, returns the final checkpoint, detaches couplers and records the entry with the domain's `stores()` when it has them. Both restart rollback history at the boundary; committed saves carry the membership list. `test_domains_join_and_leave_at_quantum_boundaries`. No coarse tier receives a retired domain's stores, and after an upstream segment retires the downstream boundary holds the last transferred discharge and temperature. |
| Unbounded checkpoint history | implemented | Baseline: `_record()` stored a full checkpoint of every domain at every quantum. `checkpoint_interval_s` (default one quantum) sets the rollback granule; `history_byte_cap` bounds the per-domain estimate from `history_bytes()` or `estimate_bytes()`; `compute_until()` refuses a projected overrun and `advance_one()` rolls back a quantum that crosses the cap; `prune()` keeps the checkpoint anchoring the first frame; `intervene()` replays from the latest checkpoint on the quantum grid. `test_sparse_checkpoints_keep_one_per_granule_and_replay_interventions` (checkpoints at 0, 5, 10 s over 13 frames; replayed intervention equals a direct run), `test_committed_save_uses_the_latest_sparse_checkpoint`, `test_history_byte_cap_refuses_a_long_horizon`. Engine-level memory with sparse checkpoints is measurement 10 of the spec. |
| No coupled domains | implemented | Baseline: no domain wrapped part of the river solver. `firehole_split.py`: `split_river()`, `RiverSegmentDomain`, `RiverInterface` (upstream export of the quantum as the downstream boundary; face dispersion `G (T_up - T_down)` at the quantum start as equal and opposite cell heat; one `FluxLedger` record per quantum that rolls back with the runtime) and `compare_split()`. `River.set_forcing(..., retain_history=False)` keeps interface schedules constant-size. Preregistered acceptance, face 137/138, quantum 10 s, step 1 s, 7200 s: build `db183e56a88e` replay max cell difference 3.9e-6 C over 720 samples, outlet water 0 and heat 3.7e-10 relative, interface water and heat 0 relative, heat budget 1.1e-14 of throughput; published Midway replay max 2.8e-13 C with the same closures; tolerances 0.02 C, 1e-9, 1e-9, 1e-6, 1e-4. Tests: acceptance face over 1800 s, a 5 C inflow front through face 19/20 over 1800 s within the same tolerances (cell 20 warms 4.98 C; worst cell difference 0.0040 C), and an intervention replay that records each transfer once and equals a fresh run exactly. The split covers heat transport only; LiveEngine ecology, solutes and actors are not split, and segment checkpoints are not persistable. |
