# Firehole simulation residency ledger

Specification: `fixes/fixes-69-firehole-simulation-residency.md`.

Baseline ledger written at document creation with every row `missing`;
rows updated with the implementation and its evidence.

| Item | Verdict | Implementation and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| No coarse tier for a river segment | implemented | Baseline: `RiverInterface.detach()` leaves the downstream boundary at the last transfer; no coarse segment model exists. `coarse_river.py`: `coarse_river()` merges fine cells into control volumes (lengths, volumes and surface areas summed, temperature volume-weighted, dispersion length-weighted) with a range starting at each lateral inflow cell, and refuses prescribed cell heat, shortwave multipliers and hyporheic zones; `restrict_temperatures()` and `prolong_temperatures()` conserve heat per coarse cell; `CoarseRiverSegmentDomain` steps the same `River` solver once per quantum and labels its snapshots `coarse`. `firehole_split.segment_river()` and `start_at()` build a segment at any frontier time; `RiverInterface` takes the face conductance from the adjacent end cells when none is given (`face_conductance()` equals the solver face dispersion on every demo face). Tests: `test_coarse_restriction_and_prolongation_conserve_heat`, `test_coarse_segment_between_fine_neighbors_closes_budget` (fine, coarse, fine over 3600 s: interface exports equal boundary inputs within 1e-12, heat closure within 1e-9), `test_end_cell_conductance_equals_the_solver_face_dispersion`. The first full walk merged the Excelsior inflow cell into a 23-cell range; installing that segment needed a 12.3 GJ reconciliation and the walker-cell difference reached 0.54 C. Starting a range at the inflow cell lowered these to 0.29 GJ and 0.014 C. |
| No anticipation set | implemented | Baseline: no module ranks domains for activation. `residency.anticipation()` ranks the reach set (`p` to `p + v * 20 s`), the stretch ahead to `v * L`, and the upstream halo whose inlet-to-tile travel time is below `L * time_scale`; `ResidencyManager.lead_wall_s()` sets `L` from the largest segment times the replay interval over the measured worker rate plus the 30 s margin. `test_anticipation_ranks_reach_ahead_and_halo` covers downstream, halo and upstream travel. |
| No admission control | implemented | Baseline: `ParkRuntime` advances every resident domain at fine resolution. `residency.admit()` admits reach segments first, holds the rate at `budget / reach_cells` when they exceed the capacity budget, and admits the rest in rank order inside it; capacity is the fine cell-simulated-seconds per wall second measured from each segment advance (`RiverSegmentDomain.last_advance_wall_s`). `test_admission_holds_the_rate_when_the_reach_set_exceeds_capacity`. |
| No worker processes | implemented | Baseline: all domains and activation work run in the runtime process. `ResidencyManager` owns a spawn-context `ProcessPoolExecutor`; `spinup_job()` rebuilds the fine segment in the worker and `replay_segment()` replays the recorded inlet discharge, inlet temperature and face dispersion per quantum from the last fine checkpoint (under one simulated day old), the published initial state, or the coarse state one spin-up interval back; stale results start catch-up jobs; installation reconciles each coarse cell's heat and records the shift. `test_worker_spinup_equals_the_in_process_replay` (bit-identical temperatures), `test_a_returning_walker_reactivates_from_the_fine_checkpoint`. Resident segments advance in the runtime process because adjacent segments exchange within each quantum. |
| Firehole walk acceptance | pass | `tools/park_firehole_walk.py`, report `evidence/park-firehole-walk.json` (input `data/river_replay.json`): six segments of 46 cells, 1.4 m/s from the upstream face to the outlet, 393 updates over 3920 simulated s, 4.2 s of wall time. 1: every segment s1 to s5 installed 10.5 to 19.2 s before the walker reached its upstream face, zero updates with the walker on coarse state. 2: s0 to s4 retired 10 s after leaving the anticipation set, at most two segments fine. 3: ten swaps, water identical, heat within 1.6e-16 relative. 4: tier heat closure 2.5e-14 of the 4.94e12 J throughput, interface faces within 2.0e-16, water within 1.1e-16. 5: demand inside the capacity budget at every update (capacity 2.9e5 cell-s/s, worker rate 1.9e5 cell-s/s). Diagnostic: walker-cell temperature against the single-domain solver, worst 0.014 C, mean 0.0072 C. Tests: `test_synthetic_walk_activates_ahead_and_retires_behind` and `test_firehole_walk_activates_downstream_before_arrival_and_retires_upstream` (to the end of s2) against the same tolerances; `pytest tests/park_runtime` 135 passed; full `pytest -q` 2306 passed, 17 skipped, 2 failed (two Godot subprocess tests past their 30 s timeout, failing identically before this change); build `c2c0632a4553` passed; `audit_provenance` 43 files, 0 failures. The walk runs at time scale 1 on a virtual wall clock; accelerated time, the service focus endpoint and Godot consumption are not exercised, and ecology, solutes and actors are not segmented. |
